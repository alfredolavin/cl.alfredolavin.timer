# Code of Conduct

## Everybody is welcome

This project is open to everyone, whatever their identity, body, background, age, ability, neurotype, language, or experience. Nobody has to earn their place here. If you are here to build, learn, or simply use what we make, you belong.

## Our values

We collaborate horizontally. No one's voice outranks another's because of title, seniority, or status, and we stay critical of authority and of the power imbalances that quietly form in any group.

- **Anti-hierarchical and community-oriented.** Decisions are made in the open, through discussion, with those affected.
- **Mutual aid.** We share knowledge, time, and credit freely. Helping each other is the default.
- **Anti-exploitation.** We do not extract from contributors or users: no coerced labor, no dark patterns, no surveillance.
- **Environmental consciousness.** We prefer software that is light on resources and hardware, and we are skeptical of technological over-reliance and unnecessary complexity. Simple beats clever.
- **Accessibility.** We design and communicate so that as many people as possible can take part: readable contrast, keyboard access, plain language, and patience with different ways of working.

## Absolutely prohibited

Harassment, hate speech, bullying, intimidation, doxxing, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated, in any form, in any space connected to this project. This includes racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, antisemitism, islamophobia, casteism, ableism, ageism, xenophobia, and every similar conduct. There is no "just joking" exception.

## Trans and non-binary people

We affirm, without reservation, the identities of trans, non-binary, and gender-diverse people. We commit to:

- Using people's stated names and pronouns.
- Centering trans voices in discussions that affect them.
- Supporting access to gender-affirming healthcare and standing against efforts to restrict it.
- Opposing all transphobia, absolutely, in words, code, and conduct.

## Against genocide, occupation, and state violence

Rooted in Jewish identity and values, this project opposes genocide in the strongest terms, everywhere and always. We stand in solidarity with all peoples facing annihilation, displacement, and occupation, and we oppose occupation and state violence against Palestinians and Indigenous peoples. Never again means never again for anyone.

## Restorative justice

When harm happens, our goal is repair, not punishment for its own sake. In order:

1. Listen to the person harmed and prioritize their safety and needs.
2. Ask the person who caused harm to acknowledge it and take concrete steps to repair it.
3. Involve the community where it helps healing and accountability.
4. If harm continues or repair is refused, restrict or remove participation to protect others.

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact the maintainer, Alfredo Lavín, at alfredolavin@gmail.com. Reports are handled with care and confidentiality, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
